2. create a clean data set with possible variables being: country, year, what industry, what percentage of GDP the industry was, what the nation did to curb the excess, what the resulting percentage of GDP it was after actions had been taken. variables to potentially use as "before" and "after" comparison:
 Export Surges ("Dumping"): When domestic markets cannot absorb the excess goods, countries often aggressively export them abroad, frequently at prices below the cost of production.
 Global Trade Frictions: A sudden spike in anti-dumping investigations, countervailing duties, and tariffs imposed by trading partners is a strong lagging indicator that an industry is operating in excess.
Capacity Utilization Rate (CUR): measures the percentage of an industry's total potential output that is actually being produced. A healthy CUR generally hovers between 79% and 85%.
